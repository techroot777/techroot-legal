# Level & Compass – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Level & Compass ("the app") is published by Techroot ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What stays on your device
The app works offline. Readings from your phone's motion and magnetic sensors, and the volume level measured by the microphone, are processed on your device only. Sound is never recorded, saved or sent: the app only calculates a loudness number from the live signal and discards the audio. We do not have an account system and we do not run servers that receive your data.

## Information collected by advertising
The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use, according to their own policies:
- Advertising ID and device information (model, OS version, language).
- Approximate location derived from your IP address.
- Interaction data with ads and basic diagnostics.

This information is used to serve, measure and personalise ads. We do not receive it and cannot link it to you. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Your choices
- In the EEA, the UK and some other regions, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time from the "Privacy settings" button in the app.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

## Permissions
- **Microphone**: only to measure the sound level in the Sound tab. It is used only while that tab is open and the app is on screen. Nothing is recorded. You can refuse or revoke it in Android Settings; the level and the compass keep working.
- **Vibration**: a short buzz when the level reaches zero, if you turn that option on.
- **Internet**: only to load ads.

The app does not request access to your camera, contacts, location or storage.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
