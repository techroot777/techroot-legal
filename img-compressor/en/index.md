# Image Compressor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Image Compressor ("the app") is published by Techroot ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What stays on your device
The app works offline. Photos and files you choose are processed on your device only. We do not upload them, we do not have an account system, and we do not run servers that receive your content.

## Information collected by advertising
The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use, according to their own policies:
- Advertising ID and device information (model, OS version, language).
- Approximate location derived from your IP address.
- Interaction data with ads and basic diagnostics.

This information is used to serve, measure and personalise ads. We do not receive it and cannot link it to you. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Your choices
- In the EEA, the UK and some other regions, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time from the "Privacy settings" button in the app.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

## Permissions
The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage. Photos are chosen through the Android system photo picker and results are saved through the system media store. The app uses the internet only to load ads.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
